# Climate & Greenhouse Gas Emission Dashboard

## Data Analyst Project

College Of Computing
Prince of Songkla University, Phuket Campus

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a **data analysis and visualization dashboard** focusing on **greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions** and **climate change impacts** global emission trends.

The project was developed as a **Data Analyst capstone project** to demonstrate how data can be used to analyze environmental problems and support sustainable decision-making.

---

## 🧠 Project Background

Climate change is primarily caused by the accumulation of **greenhouse gases** such as:

* Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)
* Methane (CH₄)
* Nitrous Oxide (N₂O)

These gases trap heat in the atmosphere, leading to **global warming** and long-term climate impacts. This project aims to help users understand the sources, trends, and effects of greenhouse gas emissions through data-driven insights.

---

## 🔍 Data & Analysis Approach

The project utilizes real-world climate and emission datasets to:

* Analyze major sources of greenhouse gas emissions (energy, transportation, industry, land use)
* Visualize emission trends over time
* Present key climate indicators using interactive dashboards

Data is presented through charts, KPIs, and visual summaries to make complex information easy to understand.
